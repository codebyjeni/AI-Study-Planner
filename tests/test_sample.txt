Expected Result

Python should be identified as having no prerequisites.
AI Basics should depend on Python.

Importance/weight (1-5): 3

Study sessions available per day: 2

Expected Result

The daily schedule should contain no more than two study sessions.
The scheduler should distribute the seven required sessions across multiple days.
All required sessions should be assigned if the overall timetable has enough available capacity and all other constraints are satisfiable.
